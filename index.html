    How to get it out

    • DropTap anywhere while the bag swings over the mug.
    • PourHold to pour and drag to aim. Pour beside the bag: a soggy bag sinks. Tap “That’ll do” when you reach the dotted line.
    • SloshSwipe left and right anywhere level with the mug. The tea rocks and the bag rides up the side.
    • SwirlDraw circles level with the mug to spin the bag. A tilted bag pokes a corner up.
    • BobTap the mug or beside it to knock it. The bag bobs up for a moment.
    • TiltOn a phone, tilt it left and right to slide the mug and slosh the tea.
    • GrabPress anywhere above the mug and drag down. The hand follows just up and to the left of your finger, so you can see what it’s doing. Pinch a corner that’s clearly out of the tea. Touch the tea and you’ll flinch and burn.
    • BrewThe dashed zone on the steep bar is a perfect cuppa. Wait long enough and the tea goes cool enough to touch (if you’ve got all afternoon), but by then it’s stewed.
