A game of thermal courage

I just usemy hands

Get the tea bag out of a freshly boiled cuppa. No spoon. Try not to cook your fingertips.

Sound on for the full sizzle.

Step one

Pick your vessel

  1. Tap to drop the bag while it swings over the mug.
  2. Hold to pour, drag to aim. Pour beside the bag: a soggy bag sinks.
  3. Let it brew. The dashed zone on the steep bar is a perfect cuppa.
  4. Swipe beside the mug to slosh it, draw circles to swirl, tap it to make the bag bob. When a dry corner pokes out, reach in and pinch it. Touch the tea and you'll know about it.
